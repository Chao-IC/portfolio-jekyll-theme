# Chao's Portfolio 

Hi, I’m Chao Yang, a London-based learner with a unique blend of expertise in data analytics, luxury brand management, and animation.

My journey began in the world of luxury, particularly in jewellery and watches, where I earned an MA in Luxury Brand Management from the University of Southampton. Currently, I’m preparing to dive deeper into the world of international marketing at King’s College London while sharpening my data analytics skills at Imperial College London. I am passionate about combining creativity, innovation, and analytical insights to deliver meaningful impact in the present.

When I’m not studying or researching, you’ll probably find me shooting with my Leica M, chasing light and moments on 35mm film.

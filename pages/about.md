# Chao's Portfolio 

Hi, I’m Chao Yang, a London-based learner with a passion for luxury consumption, digital innovation, and storytelling.

My journey began in the world of luxury, where I earned an MA in Luxury Brand Management from the University of Southampton. Currently, I’m preparing to dive deeper into the world of international marketing at King’s College London while sharpening my data analytics skills at Imperial College London. My goal? To transform data into insight-driven decisions that make a meaningful impact in the present.

I love finding the sweet spot between creativity, strategy, and data, bringing that mix to every project I work on. When I’m not studying or researching, you’ll probably find me shooting with my Leica M, chasing light and moments on 35mm film.
